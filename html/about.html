About Duct Tape Games

Duct Tape Games makes games that people play together: quick to learn, built for rematches, and better with a friend on the other side.

Who we are

Duct Tape Games Inc is a game studio based in Illinois, USA, and the developer and publisher of Battle Planes Multiplayer.

Battle Planes Multiplayer

Battle Planes Multiplayer is Avioane, the Romanian notebook game, now online. Hide three planes on a 10x10 grid and take turns firing at your opponent's sky. Every shot is a hit, a miss or a kill: find the cockpit and the whole plane goes down. Play the AI, ranked matchmaking, or a private room with a friend, free on PC and mobile.

Avioane has no single inventor. Generations of Romanian students played it on the squares of a math notebook, passing it from desk to desk. The same plane hunt is also played in China as 炸飞机 and elsewhere in Eastern Europe and Asia. We kept the notebook rules and added what paper can't: opponents worldwide, Elo rankings, and rematches in one tap.
