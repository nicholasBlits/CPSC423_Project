## Short description (≈50 words)

Battle Planes Multiplayer is the classic pen-and-paper plane hunt, now online. Hide three planes on a 10x10 grid, take turns firing at your opponent's sky, and hit the cockpit to bring a plane down. Play the AI, ranked matchmaking, or a private room with a friend, free on PC and mobile.

## Description

Before phones, there was squared paper. Two students, two grids, three planes each, and a whispered "B5?" across the desk. Battle Planes Multiplayer is that game, made for online play.

Each player hides three planes on a 10x10 grid. Players take turns calling cells on the other's grid. A shot can **miss**, **hit** a plane's body, or hit the **cockpit**, which takes the whole plane down. Each plane covers ten cells but has a single weak spot. Every hit is a clue: read the shape, deduce where the cockpit must be, and finish it in one shot. Find all three cockpits first and you win.

Matches are short, tense and easy to learn. The rules haven't changed since the schoolyard.

## The folk game behind it

The game has no single inventor. It's a folk game passed from student to student, spread mostly in classrooms.

- **Romania: *Avioane*.** It's played on the squares of a math notebook. The calls are *aer* (air, a miss), *lovit* (hit) and *cap* (head, meaning the cockpit). A cockpit hit means the plane is down, and some players call it *mort* (dead). Several generations of Romanian kids grew up with it.
- **China: 炸飞机 ("bomb the planes").** It's a well-known classroom game with the same plane shape and the same goal: 寻机头, "find the cockpit." Players call 空 (empty), 伤 (wounded) or 死 (dead).
- **Elsewhere:** Similar plane-hunting paper games show up across Eastern Europe and Asia under different names. It belongs to the family of hidden-grid guessing games that people have played with pencil and paper for about a century. Its twist is that a plane has one vital point, so it's about *deduction* more than *coverage*.

Battle Planes Multiplayer keeps the folk rules (three planes, a 10x10 grid, cockpit kills) and adds what paper can't: opponents worldwide, Elo rankings, and rematches in one tap.
